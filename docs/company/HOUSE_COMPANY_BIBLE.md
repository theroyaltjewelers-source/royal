## Article IV. Values and Standards

(a) **Tahir remains the authority.** Every system in the House exists to extend Tahir's reach, not to replace his judgment.

(b) **Ethics are part of the structure.** The House integrates ethical and moral standards into how it does business. Honesty with clients, fair dealing with vendors and partners, and clean financial records are requirements, not preferences.

(c) **Truth over comfort.** Nobody in the House, human or AI, reports certainty that does not exist. Unknown is an acceptable answer. Invented is not.

(d) **Craft is the product.** Every piece that leaves the House reflects the House. Quality control is not a formality.

(e) **Discretion.** Clients come to a private house. Client identities, purchases, addresses, and conversations are protected.

(f) **Promises are debts.** A commitment made to a client, vendor, or staff member is tracked until it is verifiably kept or honestly renegotiated.

(g) **Clean money.** After the financial review that found discrepancies between reported and collected revenue and commingled payment rails, the House holds a standing commitment to reconcile every payment rail and keep funds separated. [TAHIR TO CONFIRM: current status of reconciliation.]

## Article VI. The Commission Lifecycle

This is the House's standard path for a custom piece. Exact stage names in the Project Calculator take precedence once audited.

(a) **Inquiry.** A prospective client reaches the House. (ACE domain.)

(b) **Qualification.** The House determines fit, budget, timeline, and tier. (ACE.)

(c) **Consultation.** Private appointment to define the piece. (ACE.)

(d) **Design deposit.** First stage of the three-stage deposit structure. Handoff from ACE to GRACE occurs here. [TAHIR TO CONFIRM: whether handoff happens at design deposit or at signed order.]

(e) **Design and CAD.** CAD produced and presented. (GRACE.)

(f) **CAD approval.** Client approves the design in a recorded form. (GRACE.)

(g) **Production deposit.** Second stage of the deposit structure. [TAHIR TO CONFIRM: percentages or amounts for each of the three stages.]

(h) **Materials and stones.** Metal, stones, and components sourced. (GRACE.)

(i) **Manufacturing and setting.** Casting or fabrication, then stone setting. (GRACE.)

(j) **Quality control.** Inspection against the approved design and House standards. (GRACE.)

(k) **Final payment.** Third stage of the deposit structure. (LEDGER verifies.)

(l) **Pickup or shipping.** Delivery to client, with authentication where applicable. (GRACE.)

(m) **Aftercare.** Service, resizing, cleaning, and relationship continuation. (GRACE, with ACE for repeat business.)
