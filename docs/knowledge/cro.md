## Pipeline and stages

A pipeline lists every opportunity by stage (inquiry, qualified, consultation, proposal, deposit), with value, owner, next step and date. A healthy pipeline has a next action and a date on every opportunity.

## Qualification

Qualify early on budget, timeline, decision maker and fit with what the House does. Frameworks like BANT (budget, authority, need, timeline) keep qualification consistent. Disqualifying quickly frees time for real buyers.

## Discovery and consultative selling

Ask about the occasion, the meaning, the person, the timeline and the budget before proposing. Listen more than you speak. Clients buy bespoke work from someone who understood them.

## High-ticket and relationship selling

High-value purchases are driven by trust, so relationships, referrals and follow-through matter more than pressure. Respect the client's timeline while keeping momentum with clear next steps.

## Objections

Treat an objection as a question: clarify it, address the real concern (price, timing, trust, design), and confirm it is resolved. Price objections are usually value or timing objections.

## Follow-up

Most deals need several touches. Follow up promptly, with something useful (a sketch, a stone option, a date), and always set the next step. A lead without a follow-up date is a lead being lost.

## Closing

Close by confirming the decision and the next action: the design deposit, the signed order, the consultation date. Ask clearly.

## Forecasting

Forecast by stage-weighted probability (value times the historical close rate of each stage), adjusted for the specific deal. Track conversion rate stage to stage to find where leads fall out.

## CRM hygiene

The CRM is only as good as its data: every lead has a source, stage, value, owner and next step, updated after every touch.

## Revenue operations

Revenue operations aligns marketing, sales and client experience on one funnel, one set of definitions and shared metrics: lead source, conversion, deposit rate, cycle time, average order value.
