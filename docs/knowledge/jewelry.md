## Gold purity

Karat measures gold purity in 24 parts. 24k is pure gold. 18k is 75.0 percent gold, 14k is 58.3 percent, 10k is 41.7 percent. The rest is alloy, which sets color (yellow, white, rose) and hardness.

## Other precious metals

Sterling silver is 92.5 percent silver. Platinum jewelry is commonly 950 (95 percent platinum). Palladium is also used in white-metal pieces. Metal prices move every trading day; quotes should state how long a metal price is held.

## Weights

Precious metals are priced per troy ounce: 1 troy ounce is 31.1035 grams. A pennyweight (dwt) is 1.5552 grams; 20 pennyweights make a troy ounce. Gemstones are weighed in carats: 1 carat is 0.2 grams.

## The 4Cs of diamonds

Cut (how well the stone returns light), color (degree of colorlessness on a scale from D), clarity (inclusions and blemishes, from flawless to included) and carat weight. Grading reports from recognized laboratories document them.

## Natural and laboratory-grown

Laboratory-grown diamonds have the same physical and chemical properties as natural diamonds; they differ in origin and in price behavior. Disclosure of origin to the client is required.

## Gemstone hardness

The Mohs scale ranks scratch resistance: diamond 10, sapphire and ruby 9, topaz 8, quartz 7. Softer stones suit pieces worn with less impact.

## Manufacturing

Custom pieces are usually designed in CAD, printed as a resin or wax model, cast in metal by the lost-wax process, then cleaned, assembled and polished. Fabrication by hand is an alternative for some designs.

## Stone setting

Common settings include prong, bezel, pavé, channel and flush. Setting is skilled work and a frequent bottleneck; stones are set after casting and cleanup, before final polish and quality control.
