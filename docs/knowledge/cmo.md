## Positioning

Positioning is the place a brand holds in a chosen customer's mind relative to alternatives: for whom, what category, what difference, and why believe it. A clear position makes every message and price easier.

## Brand strategy and equity

Brand equity is the extra value clients attach because of the name: trust, desirability, willingness to pay. It is built by consistent experience, craftsmanship, story and scarcity over time, and lost quickly by inconsistency or discounting.

## Luxury marketing

Luxury sells meaning and rarity more than features. Protect price integrity; avoid frequent discounts. Show craft, provenance and the people behind the work. Exclusivity and personal relationships matter more than reach. Every touchpoint, from the first message to the box, is marketing.

## Content strategy

Choose a few themes the audience values (process, finished pieces, client stories, education), a consistent voice, and a cadence the team can sustain. Repurpose one strong piece across channels.

## Campaign architecture

A campaign has one objective, one audience, one core message, channels, a budget, a timeline and a success measure set in advance.

## Channels: organic and paid

Organic social builds trust and community slowly; paid media buys reach quickly. Creative is the largest driver of paid performance: test several concepts, keep the winners, retire the rest.

## Customer journey and lifecycle

Map how a client goes from discovering the House to inquiry, consultation, deposit, delivery and repeat purchase or referral. Retention and referral are usually cheaper than acquisition in high-ticket businesses.

## Measurement and attribution

Attribution assigns credit for a sale to the touchpoints that led to it. Last-click overstates the final touch; for high-ticket, long journeys, combine tracked links, inquiry source questions and cohort trends. Measure brand with awareness, search interest, direct inquiries and repeat rate, not only clicks.

## Audience research and segmentation

Segment by need and value, not only demographics: occasion buyers, collectors, culture-driven buyers, corporate gifting. Each needs different messages and channels.
