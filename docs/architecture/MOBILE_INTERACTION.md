# MOBILE INTERACTION

ROYAL is designed phone first and checked at 360x640, 390x844, 820x1180, 1366x768 and 1920x1080.

(a) **Touch to wake.** The whole Core is a large target. The light answers the moment a finger lands (a ripple where it touched, a soft squeeze and glow); what the touch does runs when it lifts. A long press opens no text selection or menu.

(b) **One hand.** Menu top right; spoken replies and keyboard bottom corners; typing bar at the bottom, above the home indicator (safe areas respected).

(c) **Typing.** The keyboard mark opens a single field. The page resizes with the on-screen keyboard (`interactive-widget=resizes-content`) so the field is never hidden. The field uses 16px text so iOS does not zoom.

(d) **Scrolling.** Only the column of objects scrolls, with soft fades at its edges. The page itself never scrolls sideways (checked at every size).

(e) **Menu.** A sheet from the right edge: Business or Personal, Decisions, Activity, Specialists, Systems, spoken replies, sounds, sign out. Escape or the close mark dismisses it.

(f) **Haptics.** A very short tap when a finger presses the Core, and short vibrations on wake, approval requests, completion and warnings, where the device supports them and the page has been touched, never under reduced motion.

(g) **Install.** The page can be added to the home screen and runs full screen with a dark status bar.

(h) **Keyboard users.** "/" or any letter opens typing; Escape closes the sheet, stops speech, cancels listening, or closes typing, in that order. Focus rings are lime and always visible.
