# MOBILE INTERACTION

ROYAL is designed phone first and checked at 360x640, 390x844, 820x1180, 1366x768 and 1920x1080.

(a) **Touch to wake, anywhere.** The whole screen is the target, on a phone as on a desktop. At rest the Core's canvas fills the screen. With an answer showing, the column of objects covers most of a phone's screen, so a tap on its empty space (the words, the gaps between cards) is a touch on the Core too; cards, links and controls keep their own taps, a scroll is never a tap, and selecting text wakes nothing. The light answers the moment a finger lands on the Core (a ripple where it touched, a soft squeeze and glow), and where the tap landed on an answer; what the touch does runs when it lifts. A long press on the Core opens no text selection or menu.

(i) **One ROYAL on every screen.** Phone and desktop share the Core's quality (both start on HIGH, `PERFORMANCE_BUDGET.md`), its speaking pulse (ROYAL's own voice drives the light by its loudness on every device; a device voice that reports no word boundaries gets a visible pulse at the pace of speech instead of a faint flutter), and its presence during an answer: on a phone the risen Core keeps a radius of 13.5 percent of the short side (11 percent on tablets that are not wide), large enough to see it breathe and speak. Specialist nodes are kept at least 48 px from the screen's edges so every name stays readable. Checked at 360x640, 390x844, 820x1180 and 1366x768.

(b) **One hand.** Menu top right; spoken replies and keyboard bottom corners; typing bar at the bottom, above the home indicator (safe areas respected).

(c) **Typing.** The keyboard mark opens a single field. The page resizes with the on-screen keyboard (`interactive-widget=resizes-content`) so the field is never hidden. The field uses 16px text so iOS does not zoom.

(d) **Scrolling.** Only the column of objects scrolls, with soft fades at its edges. The page itself never scrolls sideways (checked at every size).

(e) **Menu.** A sheet from the right edge: Business or Personal, Decisions, Activity, Specialists, Systems, spoken replies, sounds, sign out. Escape or the close mark dismisses it.

(f) **Haptics.** A very short tap when a finger presses the Core, and short vibrations on wake, approval requests, completion and warnings, where the device supports them and the page has been touched, never under reduced motion.

(g) **Install.** The page can be added to the home screen and runs full screen with a dark status bar.

(h) **Keyboard users.** "/" or any letter opens typing; Escape closes the sheet, stops speech, cancels listening, or closes typing, in that order. Focus rings are lime and always visible.
