# ROYAL BUSINESS REASONING

*What is built, and what is not.*

## 1. Built

(a) **Fact, analysis, recommendation, kept apart.** The cash answer (`cash_analysis`) states the calculator's figures, then what they mean, then "My recommendation:", then what ROYAL cannot see. A recommendation is never called a fact.

(b) **Limits said.** No bank feed, no expenses, no history by month: ROYAL says this is the position now, not the full story.

(c) **Conflicts surfaced** rather than resolved (`ROYAL_SYSTEM_CONGRUENCE.md`).

(d) **Concepts from the fabric** when Tahir asks about finance, accounting, operations, technology, sales, marketing or strategy, labelled as general knowledge, with House policy taking precedence.

(e) **Open questions** go to one model call over the House's verified facts, with the identity's rule that live facts come only from data.

## 2. Not built

A BusinessReasoningContext that selects dimensions (strategy, finance, operations, sales...) per question, a ROOT_CAUSE_ANALYSIS skill, and a House knowledge graph. Root-cause and trend questions ("why are deadlines slipping", "which vendors keep moving dates") need history the calculator does not yet send (stage timestamps, vendor dates); until then ROYAL answers from the current snapshot and says what it cannot see.
