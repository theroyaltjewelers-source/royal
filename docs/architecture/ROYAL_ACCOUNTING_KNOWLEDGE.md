# ROYAL ACCOUNTING KNOWLEDGE

*Source: `docs/knowledge/accounting.md`.*

Covers double-entry accounting, the chart of accounts, journal entries and the general ledger, the three statements and how they connect, accrual and cash basis, revenue recognition concepts, inventory and COGS, job costing, A/R and A/P, depreciation and amortization concepts, expense classification, bank reconciliation, month-end close, internal controls and audit trail, and sales tax, payroll and income tax as concepts.

**Not a CPA.** It is accounting literacy, not professional advice, and says so. Tax rules, rates, thresholds and deadlines change and vary by jurisdiction; a current tax question (sales tax rates, filing deadlines, estimated taxes, deductions, depreciation limits) is routed to research from official sources (the IRS, the North Carolina Department of Revenue) and the answer recommends confirming with the House's accountant. Without research connected, ROYAL says it cannot answer the current rule and offers the concept instead.

**Accounting analysis versus current tax requirement** are kept apart: the first is answered from the reference and House data; the second only from current sources.
